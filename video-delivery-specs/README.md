# Video Delivery Specs

Deliver motion content correctly across platforms: produce per-platform spec sheets, repurpose one master into every aspect ratio safely, handle captions and accessibility, and run a pre-delivery QC and collect-files pass before handover. For freelance motion designers and studios shipping finished motion to social, broadcast, OOH, and web.

## When it activates

- "What are the specs for Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube / broadcast"
- "Export settings for social" / "what aspect ratio and bitrate"
- "How do I repurpose 16:9 to 9:16" / "safe area / title-safe for vertical"
- "Pre-delivery checklist" / "collect files for handover"

## Example prompts

- "Give me the export specs for Reels, TikTok, and HD broadcast."
- "How do I cut down a 16:9 master to 9:16 and 1:1 without breaking the layout?"
- "Run me through a pre-delivery QC checklist before I hand this off."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — per-platform spec sheet, safe-area/UI danger zones, the cutdown matrix, caption and accessibility rules, and the QC checklist.
- `references/platform-specs.md` — full per-platform specs: pixels, durations, codecs, bitrates, audio, and file-size limits.
- `references/safe-areas.md` — ASCII safe-area maps per ratio with pixel measurements.
- `references/repurposing-matrix.md` — full cutdown matrix and a step-by-step 16:9→9:16 reflow recipe.
- `references/qc-checklist.md` — complete QC checklist plus the collect-files/handover packaging list and naming convention.
