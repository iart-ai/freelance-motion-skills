# Motion Pricing

Produce a defensible quote for a motion design project: choose a pricing model, run the day-rate math, account for the real cost drivers, and output a clean line-itemed estimate the client can approve. For freelance motion designers and studios pricing animation work or sanity-checking an existing number.

## When it activates

- "Quote a motion project" or "estimate this animation job"
- "How much should I charge for this video" / "price a 30-second explainer"
- "What's my day rate" / "build a quote"
- "Is this estimate fair"

## Example prompts

- "Price a 30-second flat 2D explainer with two revision rounds and paid social usage."
- "Help me set my day rate — I want £55k plus costs and bill about 130 days a year."
- "Is £3,000 fair for a detailed character-animation minute? Build the line items."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — pricing models, the day-rate formula, the six cost drivers, the line-itemed quote template, and 2026 ballpark ranges.
- `references/quote-template.md` — full quote template, payment-terms language, exclusions, and a kill-fee clause.
- `references/worked-example.md` — a complete worked estimate from brief to total.
- `references/rate-card.md` — expanded 2026 ranges by deliverable type and a day-rate worksheet.
