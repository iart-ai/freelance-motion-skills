# Creative Brief

Convert a vague client ask ("we need a cool animated explainer") into a structured, sign-off-ready creative brief, and generate the exact clarifying questions to send when the client cannot articulate what they want. For freelance motion designers and studios who need a locked brief before any design or quoting starts.

## When it activates

- "Write a creative brief" or "build a motion brief"
- "The client doesn't know what they want"
- "Turn this vague client request into a brief"
- "What questions should I ask the client" / "make a brief I can get sign-off on"

## Example prompts

- "Turn this email — 'we need something cool for our launch' — into a creative brief."
- "What clarifying questions should I send before I can scope this animation?"
- "Draft a sign-off-ready brief and flag every assumption I'm making."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — the 11 interrogation dimensions, the brief template, the "I don't know what I want" playbook, and a quick-reference table.
- `references/clarifying-questions.md` — send-ready clarifying questions per dimension plus a consolidated intake email.
- `references/vague-translator.md` — vague-adjective to concrete-question lookup and bounded-choice patterns.
- `references/worked-example.md` — a real vague ask turned into a completed brief.
