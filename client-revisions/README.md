# Client Revisions

Control revision rounds and scope creep on motion projects: define what a revision is, cap rounds in the SOW, collect feedback the right way, translate vague notes into actionable changes, and decline out-of-scope work politely as a change order. For freelance motion designers and studios protecting margin and timeline.

## When it activates

- "Stop scope creep" / "limit revision rounds"
- "Write SOW revision language" / "the client keeps asking for changes"
- "How do I say this is out of scope" / "write a change-order email"
- "Translate vague feedback" / "client said make it pop"

## Example prompts

- "Write SOW revision-cap language for two included rounds."
- "The client keeps adding small tweaks — draft a change-order email for a new scene."
- "Translate 'make it pop' and 'it feels off' into questions I can actually act on."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — the revision-vs-new-request distinction, SOW cap language, the feedback workflow, a vague-feedback translator, and the change-order email.
- `references/sow-clauses.md` — full SOW revision section, approval-gate language, and "round" definition variants.
- `references/email-templates.md` — consolidated-feedback, change-order, extra-round, and stalled-approval templates.
- `references/feedback-translator.md` — expanded vague-to-targeted feedback lookup and a "problem not fix" coaching note.
